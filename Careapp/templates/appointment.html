Appointment FAQs

Common questions about scheduling and preparing for your visit

Most new patients can be scheduled within 2-3 business days. Urgent cases are accommodated same-day or next-day whenever possible. You can also check our online calendar for immediate availability.

Please bring your photo ID, insurance card, list of current medications, and any relevant medical records or test results from previous providers. Arriving 15 minutes early will help ensure a smooth check-in process.

Yes, we offer telemedicine consultations for many types of visits including follow-ups, medication management, and certain specialty consultations. During booking, select "Telemedicine Consultation" as your appointment type.

We require 24 hours notice for appointment cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee. You can cancel or reschedule your appointment online through our patient portal or by calling our office.

Medical Emergency?

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, please call 911 immediately or go to the nearest emergency room.
