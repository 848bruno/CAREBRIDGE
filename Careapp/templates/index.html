Cardiology

Comprehensive heart care for prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of cardiovascular conditions

Our Cardiology Department provides state-of-the-art cardiac care with a focus on preventive cardiology, advanced diagnostic imaging, and minimally invasive interventions. Our team of cardiologists, cardiac surgeons, and specialized nurses work together to develop personalized treatment plans for conditions ranging from hypertension to complex heart diseases. We offer cardiac rehabilitation programs and ongoing support to help patients maintain heart health long-term.

Neurology

Expert care for disorders of the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system

Our Neurology Department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of neurological conditions including stroke, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, and migraines. With advanced neuroimaging technology and electrophysiological studies, our neurologists provide accurate diagnoses and develop comprehensive treatment plans. We also offer specialized programs for memory disorders and collaborate with neurosurgeons for cases requiring surgical intervention.

Orthopedics

Comprehensive musculoskeletal care from sports injuries to joint replacement

Our Orthopedics Department offers expert care for bones, joints, ligaments, tendons, and muscles. Our orthopedic surgeons specialize in joint replacement, sports medicine, spine surgery, and trauma care. We utilize the latest surgical techniques including minimally invasive procedures and robotic-assisted surgery to ensure faster recovery and better outcomes. Our comprehensive approach includes physical therapy and rehabilitation to help patients regain mobility and function.

Pediatrics

Compassionate healthcare for children from infancy through adolescence

Our Pediatrics Department provides comprehensive medical care for children in a child-friendly environment. Our pediatricians focus on preventive care, developmental screenings, vaccinations, and treatment of childhood illnesses. We have specialized pediatric services including neonatology for newborns, adolescent medicine, and pediatric subspecialties. Our team understands the unique needs of young patients and works closely with families to ensure the best possible health outcomes.

Dermatology

Expert care for skin, hair, and nail conditions with advanced treatment options

Our Dermatology Department offers comprehensive care for all skin-related conditions including acne, eczema, psoriasis, skin cancer, and cosmetic concerns. Our dermatologists utilize advanced diagnostic tools and offer a wide range of treatments from medical therapies to surgical procedures and cosmetic services. We provide skin cancer screenings, Mohs surgery for skin cancer removal, and various cosmetic procedures to help patients achieve healthy, radiant skin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, appointments, and healthcare processes

How do I schedule an appointment at Medilab?

You can schedule an appointment by calling our main line at +1 5589 55488 55, using our online booking system on our website, or by visiting our facility in person. Our staff will help you find a convenient time and connect you with the appropriate specialist for your healthcare needs.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, and Medicare. We also work with many regional providers. Please contact our billing department at (555) 123-4567 to verify if we accept your specific insurance plan before your visit.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

For your first appointment, please bring your insurance card, photo ID, a list of current medications, any relevant medical records or test results from previous providers, and a form of payment for any copays or deductibles. Arriving 15 minutes early will help ensure we have all necessary information for your visit.

Do you offer telehealth appointments?

Yes, we offer telehealth appointments for many types of consultations, follow-up visits, and certain specialty services. These virtual visits can be scheduled through our patient portal or by calling our office. Our staff will help determine if a telehealth appointment is appropriate for your healthcare needs.

What COVID-19 safety measures are in place?

We continue to follow CDC guidelines for healthcare settings, including enhanced cleaning protocols, mask requirements in clinical areas, and screening procedures. We also offer separate waiting areas for patients with respiratory symptoms and have implemented spacing in waiting rooms to maintain social distancing.

How can I access my medical records?

You can access your medical records through our secure patient portal, which allows you to view test results, appointment history, and communicate with your care team. For complete records or records to be sent to another provider, you can submit a records request form available at our front desk or on our website.
