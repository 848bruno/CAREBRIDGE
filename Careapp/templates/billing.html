Billing FAQs

Common questions about medical billing and payments

Even with insurance, you may be responsible for copayments, deductibles, coinsurance, or services not covered by your plan. Your insurance company determines what portion of the bill they will pay based on your specific plan benefits.

You can set up a payment plan by calling our billing department at (555) 123-4567 or through your patient portal. We offer flexible monthly payment options with 0% interest for qualified patients.

We offer financial assistance programs for qualified patients. Please contact our financial counseling department to discuss your situation and explore available options, including charity care and sliding scale fees.

Insurance claims typically take 30-60 days to process. During this time, you may see the balance on your account. Once your insurance company processes the claim, we'll update your balance accordingly and send you a new statement.
